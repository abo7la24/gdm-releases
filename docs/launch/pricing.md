# Pricing: GDM

**Free.** Chosen because this is a personal tool: no billing, no accounts and no payments in the app.

## Not done, and why

- The original's public pricing page and alternatives were **not read**, so there is no comparison table. Only what the research already holds: reviewers called the original free or cheaper than the paid IDM (a few of the 15 reviews), and the research found **no pricing or billing complaints**.
- No Stripe products to create.

## If you ever sell it

Start from: free core, one paid tier for convenience features (for example scheduling), monthly and annual (about 2 months free), one-click cancel, clear renewal emails, no surprise price jumps. Before deciding, read the original's pricing page and 2 or 3 alternatives, and record each URL and the date read.
